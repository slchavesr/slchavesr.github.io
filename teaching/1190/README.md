## Version 15 (Exam 3 A/B update)
- Expanded L'Hôpital, related-rates (angle of depression), and square-base shipping optimization variants; retained previous generators.
- Added piecewise derivative graphs, including open-circle jumps where f' is undefined, corners in f', and smooth parabola variants.
- Students enter their own increase/decrease and concavity intervals instead of selecting preprinted intervals, both in topic practice and the practice exam.
- Added free-entry critical-number classifications and inflection x-values for derivative graphs.
- Interval checker accepts union order, infinity spellings and small rounding differences.
- Note: graphical sketches are formative and scored approximately, not a rigorous assessment of calculus properties.
